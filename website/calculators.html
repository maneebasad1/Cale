How these numbers are calculated

BMR and TDEE

BMR uses Mifflin–St Jeor: 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age, plus 5 for men or minus 161 for women. Maintenance calories (TDEE) multiply BMR by activity: 1.2 sedentary, 1.375 lightly active, 1.55 moderately active, 1.725 very active, 1.9 extra active.

Weight-loss and gain targets

CALE estimates a modest weekly change from the gap to your goal weight, then converts that to a daily calorie adjustment using about 7,700 kcal per kilogram. Maintain uses your TDEE with no adjustment.
